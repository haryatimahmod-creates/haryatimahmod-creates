PhDiaries

A journey of becoming a researcher.

Research, reflections, lessons, struggles, discoveries and little wins along the way.

Not everything in a PhD journey belongs in a thesis.

Some things are learned through uncertainty, failed attempts, small breakthroughs, late nights, unexpected ideas, and moments of simply wondering, "Am I doing this right?"

PhDiaries is my space to document those moments — the research, the learning, the struggles, the discoveries, and the little wins along the way.
