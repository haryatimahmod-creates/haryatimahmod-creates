Starting My PhD

The girl who once hated computers.
October 2026 · Haryati Mahmod

I've always wanted to learn.

Maybe since I was 20, I've had this little dream of getting as much education as I possibly could. I just wanted to know more, learn more, and see how far I could go.

But honestly, my academic journey was not exactly... on schedule. 😂

I started my Diploma at 21. Then came my Degree. I was 30 when I started my Master's.

Yes, 30.

I know. Late. 😅

But strangely, I never really felt left behind.

Maybe because I learned that life doesn't come with a fixed timeline. Some people start early. Some start later. Some take a few detours before they find their way.

I was simply on my own timeline.

And Alhamdulillah, I was blessed to achieve the dreams I once prayed for.

"Life doesn't come with a fixed timeline."

After completing my Master's in 2018, I actually wanted to continue straight into a PhD.

I thought, "Okay, Master's done. PhD next."

Simple, right?

Well... not quite.

Life happened.

Plans changed. Responsibilities came. There were things I had to put first. And slowly, that PhD dream was placed somewhere in the corner of my heart.

Sometimes I wondered, "Will I ever get to do it?"

And maybe that was the hardest part.

Not knowing when.

But I never completely let go of the dream.

🌱

And now, Alhamdulillah...

I'm finally here.

Starting my PhD.

It feels exciting.

It feels scary.

And honestly, it feels a little emotional too.

Because this is something I wanted for a very long time.

So, if you're reading this, please send a little doa for me.

Pray that I'll have the strength, patience, health and discipline to finish this journey on time.

And most importantly, I hope whatever I learn along the way can eventually be given back to the students I serve.

💻

But here's the funny part...

Back in 2007, I was not someone who loved technology.

In fact... I HATED computers. 😂

When I was choosing a diploma programme, my thinking was very simple:

"Okayyyyy... let's choose something that DOESN'T have the word 'computer' in it."

So I picked...

MULTIMEDIA.

Damnnnn. 🤣

I was so clueless.

My very first class was C++ programming.

C++. ME.

A girl who barely knew how to switch on a computer.

And now my lecturer was asking me to code:

Hello World.

I was crying inside. 😭

At one point, I had to call my best friend and ask her to create an email account for me because...

"What is email?" 😂

Looking back, it is actually hilarious.

But at that time?

I was terrified.

I didn't know what I was doing.

I didn't think I was good at technology.

I didn't think this was where I belonged.

And somehow, that clueless girl kept going.

One class became another.

One assignment became another.

One semester became another.

And eventually, Multimedia became part of who I am.

Then came technology.

Then education.

Then research.

And now... Virtual Reality.

The girl who once tried to avoid the word computer is now doing a PhD exploring technology and learning.

Life is funny like that.

Maybe this is why I want to share this journey.

Because I know what it feels like to start from zero.

To feel late.

To feel clueless.

To wonder if you are good enough.

To watch other people move ahead while you are still trying to figure things out.

I know what it feels like to have a dream, put it aside, and wonder if you will ever get another chance.

"Being late does not mean you have lost."

But I also learned something.

Being late does not mean you have lost.

Starting from zero does not mean you cannot become good at something.

And taking a longer route does not make your journey less meaningful.

Sometimes, the path we didn't plan becomes the path that shapes us the most.

So this is my beginning.

My PhDiaries.

I want to share the good days, the confusing days, the little wins, the failures, the things I learn, and probably a few moments where I question my life choices. 😂

If you're someone who feels like you're starting from zero, I hope you find a little hope here.

You don't have to have everything figured out.

You just have to start.

Keep learning.

Keep exploring.

Keep going.

Because one day...

You might look back at the person who was once so scared and clueless...

and realise,

she actually made it.
Learn. Explore. Impact.
